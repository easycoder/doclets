Do I just close the Reasonix window to exit?

**Assistant**

Not quite — and `/exit` isn't a Reasonix command at all. Unknown slash commands are deliberately forwarded to the model as an ordinary turn (with an "unrecognized command" notice), which is exactly why your `/exit` landed on my desk. The real built-ins are the local ones (`/compact`, `/new`, `/clear`, `/effort`, `/mcp`, `/help`, `/model`, `/resume`, `/rewind`) — they manipulate session state and never reach the model.

On the window: your `~/.reasonix/config.toml` has `[desktop] close_behavior = "background"`, so clicking the window's close button only hides it. The Electron shell and the Go kernel stay alive, the tray icon stays, and the session keeps running — close ≠ quit.

To actually exit:
- Menu → "Quit Reasonix" (`Ctrl+Q`), or
- tray icon → "Quit" (its menu is just Open + Quit)
- Or, if you'd rather close = exit, switch the close behaviour to `quit` in General settings (the `[desktop].close_behavior` key, values `quit` or `background`).

Nothing is lost either way: transcripts and project memory live on disk, so quitting mid-session is safe — reopening and resuming picks the thread back up. I only read `~/.reasonix/config.toml`; I won't write session state, since the app owns those files and external edits just turn into conflict copies.
